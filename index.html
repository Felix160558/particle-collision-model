CORE COLLECTION · FOUR EXPERIMENTS

A concise path through
microscopic thermodynamics.

Four complementary models connect pressure, molecular reactions, energy geometry, and phase change. Move through them in order, or enter any experiment directly.

01 / KINETIC THEORY

FROM COLLISION TO PRESSURE

Single-Particle Pressure

Follow momentum transfer at a wall and see how repeated microscopic collisions become a measurable force.

OPEN EXPERIMENT → 02 / REACTION DYNAMICS

FROM COLLISION TO CHEMICAL CHANGE

Hydrogen–Iodine Reaction

Choose the H₂, I₂ and HI mixture, add energy, and count successful bond exchanges. See iodine concentration as a purple gas.

OPEN EXPERIMENT → 03 / ENERGY GEOMETRY

FROM CONSTRAINT TO DISTRIBUTION

High-Dimensional Energy Sphere

Use the geometry of a fixed-energy velocity space to build intuition for statistical energy sharing.

OPEN EXPERIMENT → 04 / PHASES OF MATTER

FROM ORDER TO DIFFUSION

Solid · Liquid · Gas

Compare particle arrangement and motion as heating and cooling carry the model through three phases.
