Model assumptions & energy accounting

H₂ + I₂ ⇌ 2HI is an effective teaching reaction, not a complete mechanism. Real molecular masses and approximate bond lengths are used. Rounded bond energies: H–H 436, I–I 151, H–I 300 kJ mol⁻¹. A forward event releases 13 kJ mol⁻¹; its reverse consumes 13. Pair momentum and kinetic plus bond-potential energy are conserved at each reaction. Rotation is visual; rotational and vibrational energies are omitted.

At the 10,000 K slider endpoint, the saved molecular mixture is replaced by H and I atoms. This is a prescribed atomic-limit illustration, not a prediction that dissociation begins or completes at this temperature. The thermostat supplies the bond-breaking energy. Lowering the temperature restores the saved molecular composition; atom recombination kinetics are not simulated. No ionization is included.

Experimental activation parameters are used as educational reference values and extrapolated outside their measured range; equilibrium composition and absolute rates are not quantitatively calibrated. NIST kinetic reference
